:::note Metric scope
Tracking Return measures pelvis-XY/yaw-aligned local-pose quality. Progress and Tracking Return use the same pelvis-height/projected-gravity termination, and reward is counted only before termination.
:::

## Protocol

- `L = LAFAN-40`, `P = PHUMA-30`, `R = Root-90`.
- `Body Pos`: mean local-body position error across the three splits; lower is better.
- `Body Ori`: mean local-body orientation error across the three splits; lower is better.
- `Global Root`: mean root-only tracking error across the three splits; lower is better.
- `GPU Hours`: training cost when recorded; populated values link to their authoritative source. Missing cells mean this audit did not find a defensible official source. The checked-in audit manifest is `docs/src/data/gpu_hours_provenance.json`.
- `Wrist Pos`: mean left/right wrist position error across the three splits; lower is better.
- `Wrist Ori`: mean left/right wrist orientation error across the three splits; lower is better.
- `Tracking Return`: mean of the three split-level normalized returns; higher is better.
- `Progress`: mean split-level motion completion; higher is better.
- All policies use seed 0, `initial_pause_s=0.0`, no runtime cap, and the same failure conditions. See [Cross-Codebase Tracking Evaluation](/tutorials/cross-codebase-evaluation) for the full definition and reproduction command.
